Building things that compound. Some take decades. Some are about catching the right wave at the right time. All of it connects.

[IT leadership by day](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tneilen/). Increasingly focused on what AI means for the messy middle. Not the frontier model hype. The existing capabilities are already mind-boggling; the gap is implementation. Scattered documents, integration friction, the workflows nobody wants to touch. That's where the leverage is. Building things on the side. Some of it might become something; most won't. That's the game.

Remote work was a deliberate choice. I do the school runs, guide the kids through their own interests, stay close to the ordinary moments. Building something for them while being there for who they're becoming.

[BJJ most days](https://www.facebook.com/p/Carlson-Gracie-Redlands-61553950796030/). What happens on the mats doesn't stay there - it's changed how I parent, work, handle hard conversations. The body and mind aren't as separate as I once thought.

The hunger for information never stops - books, podcasts, rabbit holes. But the shift now is toward creating, not just consuming. Writing more, sharing publicly, building a body of work. Input without output is just entertainment.

Coordinating [Redlands Garden Share](https://www.facebook.com/groups/redlandsgardenshare), a local community for swapping homegrown produce, seeds, and plants. Getting deeper into syntropic agroforestry - farming that builds soil rather than depleting it. The slowest game.

Based in [Mount Cotton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Cotton,_Queensland), Queensland - though increasingly curious what a less anchored life might look like.

If you're playing similar games, I'd like to hear from you - [tim@neilen.com.au](mailto:tim@neilen.com.au?Subject=Let's%20catch%20up...)
