Replace the entire "The Numbers Are Clear" section.

```
## The Numbers Are Clear

(Deloitte / Amazon, 2025)

**40hrs+**

(McKinsey Global Institute, 2024)
```

```
## The Cost of Staying Manual

**40hrs+**

(Salesforce SMB Trends, 2024)
```
