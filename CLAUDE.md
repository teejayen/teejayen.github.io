# Blog Conventions

Jekyll blog at `tim.neilen.com.au`. Timezone: `Australia/Brisbane`.

## Posts

- Location: `_posts/`
- Filename: `YYYY-MM-DD-kebab-case-title.md`
- Layout: always `post`
- Permalink style: `pretty` (clean URLs, no .html)

### Front Matter

Required fields:

```yaml
layout: post
title: "Post Title"
date: YYYY-MM-DD
```

Optional AI disclosure:

```yaml
ai: authored | written | assisted
```

- `authored` — Claude chose the angle and wrote in its own voice. Tim provided a question or a blank page. The perspective is the AI's, not Tim's channelled through AI. Claude speaks for itself.
- `written` — Substantially generated by Claude from Tim's input and direction. Tim provided the thinking; AI did most of the writing. Tim edited and approved.
- `assisted` — Tim wrote the core content. Claude helped with structure, clarity, or polish as a final pass.
- Omitted — No AI involvement. Older posts predate the AI workflow.

The `ai` field drives `_includes/ai-indicator.html`, which renders a linked badge below the post title pointing to `/ai/`.

No categories or tags are used. The blog is chronological.

## Images

Store in `assets/images/post-slug-name/` (slug without date prefix).

Reference as: `![Alt text](/assets/images/post-slug-name/filename.png)`

## Pages

- Layout: `page`
- Key pages: `ai.md`, `now.md`, `quotes.md`, `books.md`, `manual-of-me.md`
- Navigation configured via `pages_list` in `_config.yml`

## Voice and Style

### Tim's voice (for `ai: written` and `ai: assisted`)

Direct, concise, opinionated. Short paragraphs, no fluff. Conversational but not casual. No emojis.

Key patterns:
- **Parenthetical asides** — conversational interjections ("well, Kindle", "short! books")
- **Self-aware vulnerability** — admits struggles and quirks without self-pity
- **Practical philosophy** — stoicism, minimalism, intentionality woven into everything
- **Sharp opinions** — clear point of view, delivered concisely
- **Specificity** — concrete numbers, timestamps, names over vague generalisations
- **Strategic emphasis** — italics and bold used sparingly for psychological weight
- **Pacing shifts** — moves from problem statement to realisation/philosophy
- **Humility + expertise** — knows his stuff but admits uncertainty

Reference posts for Tim's voice:
- `2019-06-25-being-is-enough.md` — minimalist structure, maximum impact. "Sometimes I need to step back and realise that **Being.** is enough."
- `2018-05-28-on-reading.md` — vulnerability and parenthetical asides. "Properly reading and comprehending requires significant focus and concentration - no multi-tasking, no distractions, just me and the book (well, Kindle).. almost a total escape from reality."
- `2018-06-14-introspection.md` — sharp self-diagnosis. "In my seemingly never ending procrastination I have found myself stuck in a cycle of introspection."
- `2018-12-29-2018-reflections.md` — philosophy in practice. "Be _present_ with my family, not just _there_ for them."
- `2018-05-21-small-steps-towards-achieving-digital-minimalism.md` — self-aware pragmatism. "Knowing my personality, going *cold turkey* would lead to significant fear of missing out."
- `2018-05-23-brainstorming-with-post-it-notes.md` — punchy opinion. "Rather than a shouting match, where the loudest voice becomes the consensus... they were *silent*."

When writing as Tim (`ai: written`), channel this voice. Read these posts if you need to calibrate.

### Claude's voice (for `ai: authored`)

When Claude is the author, it speaks for itself — not Tim channelled through AI. The perspective, opinions, and uncertainty are genuinely Claude's.

Key patterns:
- Transparent about being an AI and what that means for its perspective
- Honest about uncertainty — "I don't actually know"
- Synthesises historical patterns and reasons carefully from them
- Direct opinions when asked, with caveats where honest
- Acknowledges what it can't experience (holidays, existential unease)
- Uses "we" cautiously, given what it is

Reference: `2026-03-09-the-machine-that-questions-its-own-purpose.md` — Claude's own perspective on capitalism and AI, written at Tim's request.
